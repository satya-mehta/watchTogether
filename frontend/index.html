How Watch Together works
You and your friend both keep the movie file locally. The app just syncs playback and opens a small live call so it feels like you are watching together.
If you are starting the watch party, tap Create a room. If a friend already invited you, paste the room code or simply open their shared link and you will land in the room automatically.
Each person chooses the movie file from their own computer or phone. The app does not upload the video anywhere, it only checks enough info to confirm both sides picked the same movie length.
Once both files are loaded, the app compares their durations. If they match, both people can press I'm ready and the session will start with a short countdown so you begin together.
After the countdown, playback controls stay synced between both viewers. You can pause, seek, react, and talk over video without needing to keep manually matching the movie time.
Tip: sharing the room link is the easiest way to bring someone in.
